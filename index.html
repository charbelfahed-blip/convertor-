Student trick: converting from exponent a to exponent b multiplies by 10a−b.
Example: 100 mm → dm
mm = 10−3 m and dm = 10−1 m.
Exponent difference: −3 − (−1) = −2.
100 × 10−2 = 1 dm.

Why area and volume change the exponent

For length, the prefix exponent is used once. For area it is used twice. For volume it is used three times.

1 cm = 10⁻² m
1 cm² = (10⁻²)² m² = 10⁻⁴ m²
1 cm³ = (10⁻²)³ m³ = 10⁻⁶ m³
